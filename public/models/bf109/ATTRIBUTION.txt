Messerschmitt Bf 109 G-14, 3D model

Original work: Detlef Faber and Marco Schüle, FlightGear aircraft.
Source: https://sourceforge.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/HEAD/tree/trunk/Aircraft/bf109/
License: GNU General Public License, version 2, as the README of that FlightGear aircraft says (the README and the text of the licence are next to this file).

Changes made for this site (French Touch, https://github.com/stampil/ww2):
- the AC3D model was converted by tools/convert-ac3d.py into bf109.json and bf109.bin: the discs that stand for a turning propeller and the
  high-detail glass copies are dropped, the axes are turned (x right, y nose, z up) and the model is centred vertically;
- bf109g.png is the texture of the original, shrunk from 2048 x 2048 to 1024 x 1024 pixels;
- bf109-propeller.json and bf109-propeller.bin are the three blades of the propeller of the original (Models/vdm.ac), converted the same way and placed where
  Models/vdmprop.xml puts them; vdm.png is the unchanged VDM.png of the original; the spinning discs and the bent blades are dropped, and a faint disc drawn by the site blurs the blades when the engine runs;
- the animations of the gear, the flaps, the ailerons, the elevator and the rudder are redone in Three.js from the pivots, axes and angles of Models/Bf-109g.xml;
  the interior and the weapons of the FlightGear aircraft are not included.

The model is the G-14 of 1944, the Bf 109 of the FlightGear hangar: the E of 1940 is not in it.
