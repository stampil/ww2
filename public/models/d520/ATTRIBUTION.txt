Dewoitine D.520, 3D model and cockpit

Original work: Emmanuel Baranger (helijah), "Dewoitine D520", FlightGear aircraft, 2010 to 2025.
Source: https://sourceforge.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/HEAD/tree/trunk/Aircraft/D520/
License: GNU General Public License, version 2 (the COPYING file next to this one, also the file of that FlightGear aircraft).

Changes made for this site (French Touch, https://github.com/stampil/ww2):
- the AC3D models (Models/d520.ac, Models/Interior/interior.ac, Panel/panel.ac and Seat/seat.ac) were converted by
  tools/convert-ac3d.py into d520.json, d520.bin, cockpit.json and cockpit.bin: only 29 of the 41 parts of the aircraft are
  kept (machine guns, holes, antennas, lights, pitot, high-detail glass copies and the propeller disc are dropped), as well
  as the 12 parts of the interior without the HDR copies, the panel, the seat, the collimator sight and 11 instruments
  (airspeed, altimeter, vertical speed, clock, compass, oil temperature, cylinder temperature, oil pressure, fuel pressure,
  manifold pressure, tachometer; the gear and propeller indicators are not included);
  the axes are turned (x right, y nose, z up), the origin is put on the thrust axis, and the panel and the seat get the
  offsets that FlightGear gives them;
- the animations (gear, wheels, canopy, flaps, ailerons, rudder, elevator, propeller) are redone in Three.js from the
  pivots, axes and angles of Models/d520.xml, Models/Interior/interior.xml and Models/Interior/Panel/Instruments;
- texture-francais.png is the unchanged texture.png of the original;
- texture-allemand.png is the livery "SV-GB (German)" of the original (Models/Liveries/2048x2048/SV-GB.png, signed Brett Harrison 2010 on the texture),
  with the same layout, shrunk from 2048 x 2048 to 1024 x 1024 pixels, marks unchanged;
- texture-vichy.png is that texture reworked with an image generator (1024 x 1024, same layout), after the Vichy armistice
  markings of autumn 1941 to November 1942: red and yellow bands on the cowling and the fin, a red spinner, tricolor bands under the wings;
- the textures in cockpit/ of the collimator and the instruments are the unchanged textures of the original; those of the interior, the panel and the seat
  (interior.jpg, panel.jpg, seat.jpg) replace its flat colors: the first two were generated from the original layouts, the seat is a leather picture.

These files (d520.json, d520.bin, cockpit.json, cockpit.bin, texture-francais.png, texture-allemand.png, texture-vichy.png and the cockpit
textures) are under the GPL v2, like the original.
The rest of the repository is under its own license (see the LICENSE file at its root).
The converter is in the repository, so the conversion can be redone from the original files.
